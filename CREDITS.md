# Credits

Mnemolith's models, textures, sounds and text are original work, generated procedurally by the
art/audio pipelines in `tools/` and hand-written code in this repository. No third-party models,
textures or other game assets are bundled.

If external assets are adopted in the future, each one must be listed here (and in
`docs/design/models.md`) with its source URL, author and license (CC0, CC-BY, MIT or public
domain only) before it is merged.
